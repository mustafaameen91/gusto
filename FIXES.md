# Finance & security fixes — tracker

Status: ⬜ to do · 🔄 in progress · ✅ done (tested) · ⚠️ done with a note

Snapshot of the code before any of these fixes: `backups/before-finance-fixes/`.
Each fix is tested live against `gusto_db`, which is then restored to the clean test data.

## Group 1 — Shift close and cash drawer
| # | Task | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 1 | Closing a shift posts every journal line twice and opens two new shifts | ✅ | Commit step guarded to run once (`postShift.service.js`). A shift that already has a posted entry is refused, so a double-click posts once and the second click gets "هذا الشفت أُغلق ورُحّل بالفعل" (409). Tested: 7,000 sale → 7,000 in the books, one new shift; two simultaneous closes → one posting. |
| 2 | Card sales counted as cash (drawer + books) | ✅ | Card orders are now separate: expected drawer cash counts only cash, and the books debit the bank (1102) for card, the cash box (1101) for cash. Shift screen and close summary show a new "مبيعات بطاقة" line. **Also found and fixed:** posting settings sent credit sales to 2100 الذمم الدائنة (payables) instead of 1201 ذمم مدينة - عملاء آجِل (receivables). Tested: cash 4,000 + card 14,000 + credit 5,000 → drawer expects 4,000, books 1101 4,000 / 1102 14,000 / 1201 5,000. |
| 3 | Expenses not attached to the open shift; shift expenses posted twice in the books; bank-paid expenses reduce drawer cash | ✅ | An expense paid from the cash box is attached to the open shift by the server. Only cash-box expenses reduce expected drawer cash. Shift close skips expenses already posted when created, so each is in the books once. Updating expense totals no longer wipes the shift's stored sales. Tested: sale 7,000, rent 5,000 from cash, electricity 3,000 from bank → drawer expects 2,000, closes with 0 difference, rent 5,000 and electricity 3,000 in books once each. |
| 4 | Discount on a drinks + food order blocks shift close ("Journal not balanced") | ✅ | Revenue is now each order's net amount split by that order's own drinks/food value, so the books always balance and per-order discounts land in the right account. Cancelled and returned items are left out of the split. Tested: three orders (10,000 off, 50% off, no discount) → shift closes, drinks 12,000 + food 8,500 = 20,500 exactly. |

## Group 3 — Balances and stock
| # | Task | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 7 | Customer payment does not reduce balance; allocations not validated; credit limit not enforced | ✅ | Balance = opening debt + credit orders − all payments, the same on the list and profile pages. A payment is recorded in one transaction: refused if above what's owed, allocated to the oldest unpaid orders (or to the chosen orders, validated), cash payments added to the open shift's expected drawer cash, and posted to the books (Dr cash/bank, Cr receivables 1201). Credit sales above the customer's limit are refused with a clear message (limit 0 = no limit). Tested: opening debt 10,000 + sale 24,000 − payment 20,000 = 14,000 on both pages; overpayment and wrong-order allocation refused; drawer expects the 20,000; shift closes at 0 difference; over-limit sale refused, within-limit allowed. |
| 8 | Receiving a purchase does not add stock; receive not validated / repeatable; deleting a received purchase | ✅ | Receiving now adds to stock and moves the ingredient's cost to the weighted average, in one locked transaction. The item's ingredient comes from the database (not the screen); items from another purchase, quantities above ordered, and lowering an already-received quantity are refused; re-sending the same total adds nothing. A purchase with received goods can't be deleted. Tested: 50 kg @ 2,500 + receive 10 @ 3,000 → 60 kg @ 2,583.33; repeat adds nothing; over-receive/lowering/other-purchase item refused; full receive → 70 kg, status "received"; delete refused. |
| 9 | Cost of goods sold never recorded | ⚠️ | Every sale of an item with a recipe now uses its ingredients from the kitchen internal store and records their cost (qty × average cost), and shift close posts Dr cost of goods sold 5101 / Cr inventory 1301. **Behaviour change:** food (kitchen) items used to deduct only their ready-portion count and never ingredients. They now also use their recipe ingredients, so stock the kitchen store via "نقل للمخزن الداخلي" (the test data does). **Also fixed:** size-specific recipes were never used (Cashier sends `itemSizeId`, code read `sizeId`); "harmony" items deducted an unrelated ingredient (ingredient id = menu item id), now removed. Tested: kebab + large juice → mince −0.3 kg, orange −0.4 kg, portions 50→49, cost 5,075 in books. |

## Group 4 — Orders, returns, cancellations
| # | Task | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 10 | Server trusts prices, totals and discounts sent by the screen | ✅ | The server now prices every order from the menu (base price + size adjustment), recomputes subtotal, discount and total, and stores its own figures. Discounts only from supervisor/admin (same rule as the Cashier screen); percentage ≤ 100%, discount ≤ order value. Refused: unknown/unavailable items, a size from another item, zero/negative/fractional quantities, sale types other than cash/credit. Tested: tampered 100-dinar kebab stored at 14,000; large juice 2 × 4,000; cashier discount refused, supervisor 10% → 12,600; 7 invalid orders refused; and a real order placed through the Cashier screen in Chrome (10,000). |
| 11 | Returns: unlimited refund amount, repeatable, any role, not in drawer or books | ⚠️ | A return is now an event of the shift it happens in, done in one transaction. The original order keeps its total, so closed shifts stay exactly as posted. Refund = what the customer paid for the item (price after the order's discount); the screen's amount is ignored. Can't return more than is left; the returned quantity is split onto its own line so item reports drop it. Replacements priced from the menu; if dearer, the customer pays the difference. Cash refunds lower that shift's expected drawer cash; refunds to a customer's account lower their balance. Shift close posts Dr revenue / Cr cash or receivables (replacements Cr revenue). Admin/supervisor only. The Orders page preview now shows the same amounts, including "يدفع العميل الفرق". **Not covered:** an item marked "returned to stock" doesn't reverse its ingredient cost, and a replacement dish doesn't use ingredients (only its kitchen portion). Tested: 16 checks, including 10%-discount refunds (3,600 not 4,000), over-return refused, exchange −11,300, credit-account refund, drawer 18,100, balanced books. |
| 12 | Any user can cancel items after payment; partial cancel leaves total unchanged | ✅ | Cancelling (an item or a whole order) is now its own transaction: admin/supervisor only, and only while the order's shift is open (after close, use a return). The order total drops by what the customer paid for the cancelled items, the discount in proportion, and the payment record follows. Items not started yet give back their kitchen portion and ingredients and their cost is reversed; started items count as used (their cost stays). Kitchen and drinks screens can still mark items preparing/ready/completed. Tested: cashier refused; juice cancel 15,300 → 12,600 with orange returned; cancel rest → order cancelled; shift closes with no revenue and 4,450 cost for the wasted kebab; cancelling after close refused. Full regression of tasks 1–12 passes. |

## Group 5 — Suppliers
| # | Task | Status | Notes |
|---|------|--------|-------|
| 13 | Supplier debt calculated four different ways; cash-purchase payments never recorded; negative payments accepted | ⚠️ | One definition now feeds the list, profile page and summary: opening debt + received credit purchases + service invoices − payments − supplier-tagged expenses − credits (returns/refunds now *lower* debt; they used to raise it) − transfers out + transfers in. Payments and credits must be positive; a payment can't exceed what's owed. **Books (new):** receiving posts Dr inventory 1301 / Cr suppliers 2101 (cash purchase: Cr cash box); paying a supplier posts Dr suppliers 2101 instead of an expense account (it used to count the same cost twice once task 9 posted cost of goods). Tested: 14 checks, list = profile = summary at every step (250,000 → 280,000 → 230,000 → 200,000 → 190,000), journal entries checked, 5 invalid payments refused. Full regression of 1–13 passes. |
